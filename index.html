Bindrune Forger (Horizontal Ligature)

Historical Note: This tool does not generate modern mystical or magical sigil-style bindrunes. It writes historically accurate bindrunes (bandrúnir) as horizontal ligatures sharing staves—the way Viking Age carvers genuinely wrote personal names, maker signatures, and space-saving inscriptions on wood, bone, and stone.

Guide & Help

Search: Look up Old Norse headwords or English meanings. Standard ASCII spellings (like th, dh, ae, oe, o) automatically match Old Norse characters (þ, ð, æ, œ, ǫ).

Grammar: Paste or type an Old Norse sentence to analyze word forms, parts of speech, cases, and dictionary roots.

ᚠ Fuþark: Transliterate Old Norse or Latin text into Younger Futhark (16-rune Viking Age alphabet).

ᛉ Bindrunes: Forge historically styled horizontal stave-sharing bindrunes from Old Norse words or individual runes.
