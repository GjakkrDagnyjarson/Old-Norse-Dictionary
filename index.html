Bindrune Forger (Horizontal Ligature)

Historical Note: This tool does not generate modern mystical or magical sigil-style bindrunes. It writes historically accurate bindrunes (bandrúnir) as horizontal ligatures sharing staves—the way Viking Age carvers genuinely wrote personal names, maker signatures, and space-saving inscriptions on wood, bone, and stone.
